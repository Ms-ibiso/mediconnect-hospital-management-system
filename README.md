# MediConnect Hospital Management System

## Project Overview

MediConnect is a self-directed IT project simulation based on a proposed Hospital Management System (HMS).

The project explores how a hospital could improve its daily operations through a centralised digital system for patient registration, appointment scheduling, medical records and billing.

The case study assumes challenges such as delays in patient registration, difficulty retrieving paper records, manual appointment scheduling and billing errors.

**Note:** This is a learning and portfolio project. It is not a live hospital implementation, and the challenges described are scenario assumptions.

## My Role

I approached this project from a Project Coordinator and Business Analyst perspective.

My work focused on:

- Defining the project objectives and scope.
- Identifying assumed business problems and stakeholder needs.
- Documenting business and functional requirements.
- Breaking project activities into manageable tasks.
- Developing a project timeline.
- Identifying and documenting project risks and issues.
- Planning user acceptance testing.
- Preparing deployment, training and handover documentation.
- Documenting project closure and lessons learned.

## Project Objectives

The proposed system aims to:

- Improve patient registration and record retrieval.
- Support appointment booking and management.
- Provide organised access to patient medical records.
- Improve billing accuracy and payment tracking.
- Support authorised access to information across departments.
- Provide management reports for operational monitoring.

## Project Scope

**Included in the proposed MVP:**
- Patient registration and profiles.
- Patient search and unique identification.
- Appointment management.
- Electronic medical records.
- Billing and payment records.
- Role-based access control.
- Management dashboard and reports.

**Outside the initial scope:**
- Telemedicine and video consultations.
- Ambulance tracking.
- Advanced pharmacy inventory.
- Full laboratory system integration.
- Automated insurance claims.
